## Design Decisions and Limits

Add shared tables alongside existing stores. Do not reinterpret legacy
publication dates or force vulnerability evidence into a generic backfill.
Typed subjects and separate observations cover this first slice; dedicated
entity/artifact/assessment catalogs wait for actual consumers. There is no
automatic attribution or independence score.

Separate local `capture_key` from `source_revision`. Interrupted imports
replay their persisted keys; new retrievals have new collection times and
capture keys even when the source revision is unchanged. Each access-layer
write is independently committed. Later batch completion must wait for all
intended observations and citations.

The sole starter source is the existing RVM provider. Its license finding
points to the saved 2026-11-11 research and is not newly verified. Terms remain
unreviewed and collection disabled. No absent license or terms approval was
invented. Current registry metadata can be explicitly replaced by trusted
local review; append-only review auditing and expiry checks are not built.

The gate is an integration point for future source work. Existing manual feed
and enrichment routes retain their current behavior. Registry labels do not
enforce quotas, grant operator permission or provide network isolation.
5F.2/5F.3 must register work and apply reviewed collection policies explicitly.

No raw-body archive, retention worker, scheduler, job executor, extraction
save UI, selector matcher, alert inbox, collector or AI is included. Job IDs
are opaque metadata, without a job-table FK until 5F.2 defines execution
identity. Archive byte/age budgets and sensitive-data policy must be enforced
when 5F.4 introduces storage; there is no active archive policy today.
Metadata/history is retained separately from expiring future raw bodies.
