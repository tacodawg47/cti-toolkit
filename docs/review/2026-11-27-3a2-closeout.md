repaired from committed units after interruption. Completed units are never
requested again by a retry. Captures and originating execution remain

minute precision stay literal. No source date is fabricated as a publication,
observation or collection date. Original-source hops are never inferred from
names, descriptions or domains. References are passive and unresolved unless
explicitly resolved locally.

Passwords, authentication secrets, source account records, descriptions,
logos and unknown fields are discarded. Common credential patterns in retained
metadata fail closed. Captures are explicitly sensitive and originals are
forced omitted, including under generic public archive settings. Request
metadata omits the email path segment and headers, explicitly labelling the
stored surface as redacted. Audit units retain permission/job identity and
attempt/status/completion. Errors retain fixed codes, never provider bodies.

`/breaches` shows per-address attempt/last-good age and stale/failure/incomplete
states. Paged history and capture detail show provider claims/dates, literal
hops, raw availability, original/current execution and retained inbox reviews.
Every GET is passive. HIBP data has visible linked attribution. A 404 records
no publicly disclosable result and never deletes previous claims or produces
a clean verdict. Missing sensitive/retired/opted-out results remain explicit.

continuous monitoring and independent corroboration are unassessed. More
than 100 associations fails visibly rather than silently truncating. A 404
